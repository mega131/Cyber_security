📖 Cryptographic Verification Protocol

In digital forensic proceedings, maintaining an unbroken Chain of Custody requires demonstrating that the evidence examined is bit-for-bit identical to the evidence seized.

The Avalanche Effect: Modifying even a single bit in a multi-gigabyte file alters on average 50% of the output bits in the SHA-256 digest, guaranteeing immediate detection of tampering.

Forensic Reminder: A match verifies preservation since intake. It does not prove that the evidence was not forged prior to intake.
