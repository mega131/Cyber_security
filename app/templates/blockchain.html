⚖️ Why Blockchain in Digital Forensics?

In digital forensics, a common vulnerability of traditional databases (like MySQL or SQLite) is that a rogue database administrator, investigator, or compromised account can rewrite records directly without leaving a trace. By anchoring evidence into an immutable Blockchain Ledger, each block incorporates the cryptographic hash of its predecessor. If anyone alters even a single bit of evidence in Block #2, the hash of Block #2 changes, immediately breaking the link to Block #3 and invalidating the entire subsequent chain.
